The Operations Inbox gives you a live window into every SMS conversation your AI bot is managing. From a single screen you can read ongoing threads, reply manually, and take over from the bot whenever a patient needs a human touch. Navigate to `/live/inbox` to open it.

<Note>
  The Inbox is the only place in Fit4Life OS where you can monitor and intervene in live bot conversations. Bookmark it so your front-desk team can reach it instantly.
</Note>

## What you see

The Inbox is split into two panes.

**Thread list (left pane)**

Every active SMS thread appears as a row showing:

- **Customer name** — pulled from the customer record linked to that phone number.
- **Phone number** — the customer's 10-digit number, which is also the unique thread key.
- **Last message preview** — the most recent inbound or outbound message, truncated to one line.
- **AI / Human status indicator** — a badge showing whether the bot is currently in control (`AI`) or a staff member has taken over (`Human`).

**Message pane (right pane)**

Select any thread in the left pane to load the full conversation history on the right. Messages are ordered oldest-to-newest, and the pane auto-scrolls to the bottom so the latest exchange is always visible.

## Real-time updates

The Inbox polls for new messages continuously. Incoming replies and outbound bot messages appear without requiring a page refresh. The thread list re-sorts automatically so the most recently active conversation floats to the top.

## Finding a specific customer's thread

Use the search bar at the top of the thread list to filter by customer name or phone number. Because threads are keyed to the customer's 10-digit phone number, searching by phone is the fastest way to locate an exact record.

## Sending a manual reply

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the thread">
    Click the customer's row in the thread list to open their conversation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compose your message">
    Type your reply in the text box at the bottom of the message pane.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Click **Send** or press **Enter**. Your message is delivered immediately and appended to the conversation history.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## AI vs. human control

Each thread displays a status badge indicating who is currently driving the conversation.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="AI" icon="robot">
    The bot is handling replies automatically. Incoming messages are processed and responded to without staff involvement.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Human" icon="user">
    A staff member has taken over. The bot will not send automated replies to this thread until control is returned to AI.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

To transfer control, use the **Take Over / Return to AI** toggle in the message pane header. Transferring to Human pauses all bot replies for that thread immediately.

## Handling send failures

If a message cannot be delivered, the thread shows a **Send failed** banner beneath the affected message. This typically indicates a carrier issue or an invalid number.

To retry:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Locate the failed message">
    Open the thread. Failed messages are marked with a red error indicator.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Retry">
    Select **Retry** next to the failed message. Fit4Life OS will attempt delivery again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify delivery">
    A successful retry removes the error indicator and updates the last-message timestamp.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If retries continue to fail, confirm the customer's phone number is correct in their customer record and that your SMS provider connection is active in your account settings.
