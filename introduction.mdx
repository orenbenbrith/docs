Fit4Life OS is the all-in-one practice management platform designed specifically for physician-supervised peptide therapy clinics. It replaces the manual work — the 13-tab Google Sheets, iMessage reorder threads, and hand-called Ubers — with a unified dashboard that tracks customers, automates reorders, manages finances, and lets you take over any conversation with one click.

## What Fit4Life OS does

The platform covers every part of your daily workflow in one authenticated portal at `/live`:

- **Customer management** — add, edit, and search your client roster; view lifetime value, order history, and reorder cadence per customer
- **AI SMS bot** — customers text your clinic number to browse the menu, ask questions, and place reorders; the bot handles it all automatically
- **Order management** — a full order board with status tracking from "requested" through "fulfilled"; all orders are payment-gated so nothing commits without payment
- **Financial tracking** — P&L with editable per-product costs, daily sales ledger, payroll tracking, and revenue goals
- **Human takeover** — jump into any bot conversation instantly from the Operations Inbox; the bot steps aside the moment you take control
- **Reports & exports** — branded PDF, DOCX, XLSX, PPTX, CSV, and PNG exports of any data view
- **In-OS AI assistant** — a floating copilot that reads your data and proposes actions you confirm before anything changes
- **Referral tree** — visualize your referral network with revenue rollups per branch

## Getting started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to the OS">
    Navigate to your clinic's Fit4Life OS URL and enter your practice password to access the dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your customers">
    Import or manually add your client roster with names, phone numbers, and order history.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate the SMS bot">
    Connect your Twilio number so customers can text to browse the menu and place reorders automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor from the inbox">
    Watch live conversations in the Operations Inbox and take over any thread whenever you want a personal touch.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The SMS bot requires an active Twilio number and a completed A2P 10DLC campaign registration before it can send messages to real customers. See the [SMS Bot overview](/sms-bot/overview) for setup details.
</Note>
