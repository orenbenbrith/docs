Before you can manage customers, place orders, or review revenue, you need to sign in to your clinic's Fit4Life OS dashboard. Access is protected by a single practice password that your admin sets during onboarding. This page covers the login flow from start to finish, explains how long your session stays active, and introduces the navigation tools you'll use every day.

## Logging in

<Steps>

  <Step title="Open your clinic URL">

    In any modern browser, navigate to your Fit4Life OS address. Your URL ends in `/live` — for example, `https://dashboard.yourclinicdomain.com/live`. If your clinic uses a custom domain, use that address instead. Ask your admin if you're not sure which URL to use.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter the practice password">

    The `/login` page loads automatically when you visit the dashboard URL without an active session. Type your **practice password** into the password field and click **Enter Dashboard**.

    There are no individual usernames — all operators at your clinic share the same practice password. Keep this password secure and avoid sharing it outside your clinical team.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Reach the main dashboard">

    After a successful login you are redirected to the **Dashboard** home page. The Needs You triage strip, KPI tiles, and revenue chart are immediately visible. You are now fully authenticated and can navigate anywhere in the platform.

    <Info>
      Your session stays active for **24 hours** from the moment you log in. After that, Fit4Life OS automatically signs you out and redirects you to the `/login` page. Simply enter the practice password again to resume where you left off. There is no "remember me" option — this timeout is a security measure to protect patient data.
    </Info>

  </Step>

</Steps>

## Using the command palette

The command palette lets you jump to any page, customer record, or order instantly — no clicking through menus required.

<Tip>
  Press **⌘K** on Mac or **Ctrl K** on Windows to open the command palette from anywhere in the dashboard. Start typing a customer name, a page title, or an action (like "new order") and select the result to navigate there immediately.
</Tip>

The command palette is especially useful when you're on a call with a patient and need to pull up their record quickly without interrupting the conversation.

## Switching between light and dark mode

Fit4Life OS supports both light and dark themes. Click the **sun/moon icon** in the top bar (top-right corner of the screen) to toggle between the two. Your preference is saved in your browser and persists across sessions on the same device.

## If you're locked out

If you cannot log in — for example, because the practice password has changed or you've forgotten it — contact your clinic admin directly. Fit4Life OS does not have a self-service password reset flow; only an admin with access to the Settings page can update the practice password and share it with the team.

<Warning>
  Never attempt to share the practice password over unencrypted channels such as SMS or email. Ask your admin to deliver it securely (for example, via a password manager or an encrypted messaging app).
</Warning>
